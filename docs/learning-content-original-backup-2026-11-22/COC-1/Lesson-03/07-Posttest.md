# COC 1 — Lesson 3: Disassembly and Assembly of Computer Hardware

## Posttest

LESSON 3 – POST-TEST (10 ITEMS)

Directions: Read each question carefully. Choose the letter of the best answer.

1. Why should a technician wear a grounded antistatic wrist strap and handle components by their edges?

A. To increase CPU speed

B. To prevent electrostatic damage to components

C. To make RAM faster

D. To clean dust from the case

2. Why should a technician take pictures before removing internal cables?

A. To remember where each cable connects

B. To make the cables longer

C. To save disk space

D. To increase RAM capacity

3. Why should screws be placed in small containers during disassembly?

A. To make them heavier

B. To clean them

C. To make them magnetic

D. To keep them organized and prevent loss

4. What is the correct way to install a RAM module?

A. Force it into any slot

B. Install it upside down

C. Align the notch and press evenly until the retaining clips lock

D. Bend the module to fit

5. Which cable supplies main power to the motherboard?

A. SATA data cable

B. 24-pin power cable

C. HDMI cable

D. LAN cable

6. Where should the CPU cooler's fan cable be connected?

A. CPU_FAN header

B. Front audio pins

C. Hard drive

D. Monitor port

7. What is installed in the case before the motherboard so that the board does not touch the metal case?

A. Thermal paste

B. CMOS battery

C. Motherboard standoffs

D. Monitor cable

8. What should be checked during initial testing after assembly?

A. The color of the case

B. The wallpaper

C. The mouse brand

D. POST, BIOS/UEFI access, and detection of the CPU, RAM, and storage

9. A computer restarts repeatedly. Which are possible causes?

A. Overheating, unstable power, or a RAM problem

B. A wrong wallpaper

C. A missing mouse pad

D. A dirty monitor screen

10. During troubleshooting, how should possible causes be tested?

A. All at once

B. One possible cause at a time

C. Randomly

D. Only after replacing the whole computer

Answer Key: 1. B   2. A   3. D   4. C   5. B   6. A   7. C   8. D   9. A   10. B
