# COC 1 — Lesson 3: Disassembly and Assembly of Computer Hardware

## Video Lessons

1. **[How to Build a PC, the last guide you’ll ever need! (2024 Update) — Linus Tech Tips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s1fxZ-VWs2U)**  
   Detailed practical demonstration of component preparation and installation, including CPU, RAM, storage, cooler, motherboard, power supply, graphics card, cabling, first power-on, and basic troubleshooting. Use the relevant chapters rather than requiring students to watch the entire long video.

2. **[How To Build A PC — Step by Step (Full Build Guide) — TechSource](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PXaLc9AYIcg)**  
   Shows a complete desktop assembly, cable connections, first checks, Windows installation, and driver installation. Use as a second visual reference; hardware models may differ from the equipment in the laboratory.

3. **[Computer Basics: Inside a Computer — GCFLearnFree](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HB4I2CgkcCo)**  
   Helps learners identify major internal components before they begin disassembly and assembly.

### Safety reminder
These videos are demonstrations, not permission to copy every action without supervision. Before servicing: shut down, unplug AC power, disconnect peripherals, follow the device manufacturer's service instructions, use ESD precautions, and never open a power-supply enclosure. Laptop disassembly is model-specific and should use the manufacturer's service manual.
