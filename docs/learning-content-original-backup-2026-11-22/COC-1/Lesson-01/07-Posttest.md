# COC 1 — Lesson 1: Fundamental Concepts of Computer Systems Servicing

## Posttest

LESSON 1 – POST-TEST (10 ITEMS)

Directions: Read each question carefully. Choose the letter of the best answer.

1. Which group contains only output devices?

A. Monitor, printer, and speakers

B. Keyboard, mouse, and scanner

C. CPU, GPU, and motherboard

D. HDD, SSD, and USB flash drive

2. What is the function of an operating system?

A. Only edits documents

B. Manages hardware and software resources

C. Only prints files

D. Replaces the motherboard

3. What is the purpose of a device driver?

A. Stores files permanently

B. Removes dust from components

C. Allows hardware devices to communicate with the operating system

D. Measures voltage

4. Why should a technician never open the PSU casing?

A. It has no screws

B. It is made of glass

C. It contains only paper

D. Internal capacitors may retain dangerous voltage

5. Loose cables that may cause tripping are an example of which hazard?

A. Physical hazard

B. Chemical hazard

C. Fire hazard

D. Ergonomic hazard

6. Which equipment is used to check the basic output of a power supply?

A. Microfiber cloth

B. Power supply tester

C. Compressed air

D. Flathead screwdriver

7. Using the sample price list (OS Reinstallation/Reformat ₱600 and SSD Installation ₱350), what is the total labor fee for both services?

A. ₱850

B. ₱900

C. ₱950

D. ₱1,000

8. Which business cost factor is the amount added to a service price to cover business profit?

A. Warranty

B. Labor cost

C. Parts cost

D. Profit margin

9. What should a technician do before accessing or erasing a customer's files?

A. Copy them to a personal USB drive

B. Ignore the files

C. Obtain the customer's permission

D. Share them online

10. Which of the following is a possible career pathway in Computer Systems Servicing?

A. Pastry chef

B. Lifeguard

C. Pilot

D. IT support specialist
