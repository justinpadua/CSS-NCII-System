# COC 1 — Lesson 1: Fundamental Concepts of Computer Systems Servicing

## Definition of Terms

LESSON 1 – DEFINITION OF TERMS

Study these key terms before starting the lesson.

Computer Systems Servicing (CSS) – The installation, maintenance, repair, configuration, and troubleshooting of computer systems and networks.

Input Device – Hardware used to enter data or commands into a computer, such as a keyboard, mouse, scanner, microphone, or webcam.

Output Device – Hardware that presents processed information to the user, such as a monitor, printer, speakers, or projector.

Processing Device – A component that executes instructions and processes data, such as the CPU, GPU, and motherboard.

Storage Device – Hardware that keeps data, such as an HDD, SSD, USB flash drive, or memory card.

System Software – Software that manages the computer's hardware and provides a platform for application software.

Application Software – Programs that help users perform specific tasks, such as word processing, calculation, or web browsing.

Device Driver – Software that allows the operating system to communicate with a hardware device.

Firmware – Low-level software that controls and manages hardware devices.

Utility Software – Software that maintains and protects the computer system.

Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) – Rules and practices that protect workers from accidents, injuries, and health hazards in the workplace.

Hazard – Anything in the workplace that may cause injury, illness, or damage, such as electrical, ESD, physical, chemical, ergonomic, or fire hazards.

Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) – A sudden transfer of static electricity that may damage sensitive electronic components.

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) – Items worn or used for protection, such as an antistatic wrist strap, ESD mat, safety goggles, gloves, and a dust mask.

Digital Multimeter – A testing tool that measures voltage, resistance, and current.

Power Supply Tester – A device that checks the basic output conditions of a PSU.

POST Diagnostic Card – A card that displays diagnostic codes during startup to help identify motherboard or startup problems.

Business Costing – The process of calculating the expenses and service charges involved in computer servicing.

Labor Cost – Payment for the technician's time and skills.

Parts Cost – The price of replacement components.

Operating Expenses – Regular business expenses such as electricity, rent, and internet.

Profit Margin – The amount added to the service price to cover business profit.

Warranty – A service guarantee that may affect the price of a repair.

Computer Repair Shop – A business that provides computer maintenance, troubleshooting, repair, installation, and upgrade services.

Fundamental Concepts of Computers (Software and Hardware PC)

Types of Computer Hardware

Input Devices

Keyboard – used to enter letters, numbers, and commands.

Mouse – used to control the pointer and select items on the screen.

Scanner – converts printed documents into digital files.

Microphone – records voice and sound.

Webcam – captures images and videos.

Processing Devices

CPU (Central Processing Unit) – executes instructions and processes data.

GPU (Graphics Processing Unit) – processes graphics, videos, and visual information.

Motherboard – connects and allows communication between components.

Storage Devices

HDD – stores files using magnetic disks.

SSD – stores files using flash memory and provides faster data access.

USB Flash Drive – portable storage device used to transfer files.

Memory Card – small storage device commonly used in cameras and mobile devices.

Output Devices

Monitor – displays visual information.

Printer – produces hard copies of documents.

Speakers – produce audio output.

Projector – displays computer output on a larger screen.

Types of Computer Software

System Software

Operating System – manages the computer's hardware and software resources.

Device Drivers – allow hardware devices to communicate with the operating system. It helps the software to work and connect with hardware.

Utility Software – maintains and protects the computer system.

Firmware – provides low-level instructions for hardware operation. It controls and manages hardware devices.

Application Software

Microsoft Word – creates and edits documents.

Microsoft Excel – performs calculations and organizes data.

Google Chrome – accesses websites and online resources.

Adobe Photoshop – edits and manipulates images.

VLC Media Player – plays audio and video files.
