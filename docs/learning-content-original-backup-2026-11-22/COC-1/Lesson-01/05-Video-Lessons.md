# COC 1 — Lesson 1: Fundamental Concepts of Computer Systems Servicing

## Video Lessons

The following videos support the computer-fundamentals and servicing portions of this lesson. Watch the sections that match the topic being discussed.

1. **[How does a PC work and what does each part do? | Computer components explained — Nate Gentile](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0zkX6nlpiSk)**  
   Covers the CPU, storage, RAM, motherboard, power supply, cooling, graphics card, and case. Use for the computer fundamentals and hardware overview. The presentation is in Spanish; use English subtitles if available.

2. **[How to Build a PC, the last guide you’ll ever need! (2024 Update) — Linus Tech Tips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s1fxZ-VWs2U)**  
   Useful for seeing a prepared work area and careful handling of computer parts during a build. Relevant sections include work-area setup (around 38:10), component installation, and powering on. This is a long, detailed demonstration; the instructor should select relevant sections.

3. **[Computer Basics: Inside a Computer — GCFLearnFree](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HB4I2CgkcCo)**  
   Short beginner-friendly overview of the motherboard, CPU, RAM, storage, expansion slots, and power supply.

### Instructor notes
- These videos support hardware fundamentals and practical servicing context; they do **not** fully teach Philippine OHS requirements, ESD procedures, or computer-repair business costing.
- Teach and assess OHS, electrical safety, ESD precautions, and safe tool handling using the lesson discussion and instructor demonstration. Do not treat watching a PC build video as a substitute for safety instruction.
- No dedicated, verified video specifically covering the lesson's local service-pricing and small-shop costing topics was identified in this search. The instructor should teach those using locally relevant sample costs and business scenarios rather than inserting an unrelated video.
