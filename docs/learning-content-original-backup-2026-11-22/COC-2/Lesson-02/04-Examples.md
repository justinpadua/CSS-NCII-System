# COC 2 — Lesson 2: Network Installation and Configuration

## Examples

Example: making a straight-through cable

Measure the required cable length, allowing a little slack without creating a coil or trip hazard.

Cut the cable squarely. Strip only enough outer jacket to arrange the pairs without damaging insulation.

Untwist and arrange the eight conductors in T568B order. Keep untwisting to the minimum needed.

Trim the conductors evenly, insert them fully into the RJ-45 plug, and check that the jacket is captured by the plug's strain-relief area.

Crimp the plug using the correct tool. Repeat the same pinout at the other end.

Test the cable with a cable tester. Confirm that pins 1 through 8 map correctly and that there are no opens, shorts, or crossed conductors.

Label the cable and install it along the planned route using suitable clips or raceway.

Important: A basic continuity tester can identify many wiring faults, but it does not certify cable performance at a particular Ethernet category or data rate.

Example: small office Wi-Fi setup

A small office has a router at 192.168.1.1, DHCP enabled for ordinary client devices, and an SSID named Office-Lab. The administrator selects WPA2-AES or WPA3, creates a strong unique passphrase, and tests a laptop's connection. The laptop should receive an address in the planned subnet, reach the gateway, and resolve domain names if internet/DNS service is available.
