
# COC 2 — LESSON 1: Activity

## Lesson 1 — Activity: Cable It Right

**Objective:** Identify common network components, apply the T568B pin sequence, and describe safe cable-installation practices.

**Materials:** This activity may be completed on the worksheet or in a document editor. Hands-on cable work is optional and requires instructor approval, suitable cable/plug/crimping tools, and a cable tester.

**Safety:** Use only authorized equipment and an approved work area. Do not modify a live/shared network. Keep the area dry, avoid trip hazards, use eye protection when the task requires it, and follow instructor directions for tools and ladders. A paper simulation is acceptable.

**How to use:** Choose or type your response in each field; tick every safe practice that applies.
