# COC 2 — Lesson 1: Network Concepts and Planning

## Definition of Terms

- **Computer network:** Connected devices that exchange data or share resources.
- **LAN:** Local area network, a network covering a limited site such as a room, lab, or building.
- **WAN:** Wide area network, a network that connects sites over a larger geographic area.
- **Network topology:** The arrangement of network connections, physically or logically, such as a star layout.
- **Peer-to-peer network:** A model where endpoints can share resources directly without relying on a dedicated central server for every service.
- **Client-server network:** A model where client devices request services or resources from server systems.
- **Network interface card (NIC):** A wired or wireless interface that lets a device communicate on a network.
- **Switch:** A device that forwards traffic among devices on a LAN.
- **Router:** A device that forwards traffic between IP networks; consumer models may combine other functions.
- **Wireless access point (AP):** A device that provides Wi-Fi access to a network.
- **Network plan:** A documented description of devices, links, addressing, requirements, and installation considerations.
