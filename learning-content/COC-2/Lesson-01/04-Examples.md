# COC 2 — Lesson 1: Network Concepts and Planning

## Examples

### Plan a small computer laboratory network

A laboratory has six student computers, one instructor computer, a printer, and a connection to the school's authorized network. The planner records each device and location, asks which devices need to share the printer, and confirms the approved internet and security requirements. A star layout with a switch near the equipment is considered because each wired endpoint can connect back to the switch; the final choice depends on the room and available equipment.

The plan labels the router or gateway, switch, computers, printer, cable routes, and wireless access point if one is needed. It lists the cable and compatible termination parts, avoids trip hazards and crushing or sharp bends, and notes that an administrator must approve addressing and shared-network changes before installation.
