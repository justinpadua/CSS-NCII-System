### Instructor notes
- Use the same T568A or T568B standard at both ends for a straight-through cable. Traditional crossover wiring uses different standards at opposite ends; Auto-MDIX on many modern devices often makes crossover cables unnecessary.
- Router menus vary by manufacturer. Demonstrate using a lab router and reset it after practice.
- Do not publish real router-admin credentials, Wi-Fi passwords, or student network details in submitted work.
