# COC 1 — Lesson 1: Fundamental Concepts of Computer Systems Servicing

## Examples

### Prepare a small repair-service quotation

A customer authorizes an SSD installation. The technician confirms the SSD model is compatible, explains the work, and separates the part price from the labor fee. Using the discussion's illustrative sample list, the quotation can show the SSD part, SSD installation labor, and any agreed consumables as separate lines. The technician adds the lines, checks the arithmetic, states that the listed prices are examples rather than required market rates, and obtains approval before work begins.

The customer is the peopleware in the service interaction; the computer and SSD are hardware; the operating system and diagnostic utilities are software; and the backed-up files and service record are data. The technician protects customer data and records the approved work.

### Set up a safe work area

Before opening a desktop, the technician saves work, shuts down the operating system, switches off and disconnects AC power, and follows the equipment's service instructions. The technician uses an ESD-safe work surface and properly connected wrist strap, organizes screws, and keeps liquids away. The power-supply enclosure stays closed; it is not a user-serviceable part.
