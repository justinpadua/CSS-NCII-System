# COC 1 — Lesson 1: Fundamental Concepts of Computer Systems Servicing

## Definition of Terms

- **Computer Systems Servicing (CSS):** Work that installs, configures, maintains, and troubleshoots computer systems and related equipment.
- **Hardware:** Physical computer components that can be handled, such as a keyboard, motherboard, or SSD.
- **Software:** Programs and instructions executed by a computer.
- **System software:** Software, including an operating system and drivers, that manages computer resources and supports applications.
- **Application software:** Programs used to perform user tasks, such as writing documents or browsing the web.
- **Device driver:** Software that enables an operating system to communicate with a device.
- **Firmware:** Software stored in or closely associated with hardware that helps control its operation.
- **Occupational health and safety (OHS):** Workplace practices intended to reduce risks of injury, illness, and property damage.
- **Hazard:** A condition or item that can cause harm, such as exposed electrical parts or a trip hazard.
- **Electrostatic discharge (ESD):** A sudden transfer of static charge that can damage electronic components.
- **ESD protection:** Equipment and handling practices, such as an approved mat and properly grounded wrist strap, used to reduce ESD risk.
- **Labor cost:** The charge for a technician's time and work.
- **Parts cost:** The price of replacement components used in a service.
- **Operating expenses:** Ongoing business costs such as rent, electricity, and internet service.
- **Profit margin:** The part of a selling price remaining after applicable costs, according to the business's pricing method.
- **Warranty:** A stated commitment to repair, replace, or support a product or service under defined conditions.
