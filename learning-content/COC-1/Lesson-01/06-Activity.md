# COC 1 — Lesson 1: Fundamental Concepts of Computer Systems Servicing

## Activity

LESSON 1 – ACTIVITY: SAFE SHOP, SMART SERVICE

**Learning objective:** Identify appropriate servicing tools, sequence safe internal cleaning, recognize shop-readiness items, and calculate a sample labor charge.

**Materials:** Worksheet and pen. Optional instructor demonstration: an unplugged desktop system unit, anti-static brush, wrist strap, tweezers, digital multimeter, and PSU tester. Paper-only completion is acceptable. Do not open a PSU enclosure or probe live equipment.

Practice the tools, safety steps, and costing you learned in this lesson.

How to use: click each gray field in Microsoft Word to pick an answer from the drop-down list, tick a box, or type your response.

Part A – Match the Tool

Choose the tool that fits each description.  Word bank: Anti-static brush • Antistatic wrist strap • Digital multimeter • Hammer • Power supply tester • Tweezers

1. Measures electrical quantities such as voltage, resistance, or current when the correct mode and safe procedure are used.
2. Helps check basic PSU output conditions using its intended connectors and instructions.
3. Worn and connected to an approved ESD-safe ground to reduce electrostatic charge on the technician.
4. Helps handle very small screws or jumpers.
5. Used to remove loose dust from suitable internal surfaces with an appropriate safe technique.

Part B – Order the Steps

Number the steps from 1 to 5 for safely cleaning inside a PC.

- Clean loose dust using an anti-static brush and suitable safe technique; avoid forcing or damaging components.
- Shut down the computer using its operating system.
- Open the case panel after power has been disconnected and the work area is ready.
- Disconnect the power cable and other external cables.
- Put on a properly grounded antistatic wrist strap before handling internal components.

Keep the computer disconnected from power before opening it. Use only the instructor-approved cleaning method; do not use liquids or open the PSU.

Use the sample price list: RAM Installation ₱200, Driver Installation ₱200, SSD Installation ₱350.

1. Compute the total labor fee for these three services. Show your computation.

2. Write one safety rule you will always follow in the shop and explain why.

**Steps:** Complete the matching and sequencing independently, then complete Parts C and D. For an optional demonstration, obtain instructor approval, identify the test unit, isolate power, follow the lab ESD procedure, and keep the PSU closed. Record one hazard prevented by the sequence.

**Expected output:** Five tool matches, the ordered five-step cleaning procedure, Part C selections, and Part D computation and safety response.
