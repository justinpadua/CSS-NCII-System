### Safe testing reminders
- Inspect cooling, fans, heatsinks, and ventilation before applying a heavy load.
- Monitor temperatures and system behavior continuously. Stop if temperatures exceed the component manufacturer's limits, cooling fails, unusual smells/noises occur, or the system becomes unstable.
- Do not assume that a single stress test proves a computer is completely fault-free. Combine appropriate tests with visual inspection and normal-use checks.
- Record the test name, version, duration, hardware, observed temperatures, errors, and result. Do not copy a test duration from a video as a universal requirement.
