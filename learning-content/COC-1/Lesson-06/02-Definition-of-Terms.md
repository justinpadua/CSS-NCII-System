# COC 1 — Lesson 6: Computer Testing and Documentation

## Definition of Terms

- **Stress test:** A selected workload applied to a component or system to observe behavior under load.
- **Burn-in test:** A sustained operating period under a defined workload to observe stability over time.
- **Baseline:** Initial readings and system details recorded before a test for later comparison.
- **Thermal throttling:** A performance reduction used by a component or system to manage temperature or power conditions.
- **Artifact:** An unexpected visual defect, such as corruption or flickering, that may occur during graphics output.
- **Fault isolation:** Testing one likely cause or component at a time to narrow a problem.
- **Test log:** A record of test equipment or software, settings, duration, observations, results, and actions.
- **Pass under test conditions:** A result indicating no issue was observed for the specified test and period; it does not prove that no fault exists.
