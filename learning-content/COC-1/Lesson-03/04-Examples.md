# COC 1 — Lesson 3: Disassembly and Assembly of Computer Hardware

## Examples

### Investigate a desktop that powers on but shows no display

A technician first asks what changed and records the symptom. After confirming the monitor has power and the correct input is selected, the technician checks that the display cable is connected to an output supported by the installed graphics hardware. If the problem remains, the technician shuts down and disconnects power before opening the case, applies ESD controls, and checks component seating according to the service manual. Only one likely cause is tested at a time, and every change is documented. The technician does not replace parts or erase data without diagnosis and authorization.

For an assembly exercise, the trainee checks the motherboard manual for compatible CPU, RAM slots, cooler mounting, and required power connectors. The trainee aligns each part correctly and stops if a component does not fit without force.
