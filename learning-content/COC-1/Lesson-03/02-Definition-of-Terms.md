# COC 1 — Lesson 3: Disassembly and Assembly of Computer Hardware

## Definition of Terms

- **System unit:** The computer enclosure that contains internal components; it does not include external devices such as the monitor.
- **Motherboard:** The main circuit board that connects components and provides communication paths.
- **CPU socket:** The motherboard connector designed for a compatible processor.
- **RAM slot:** A motherboard connector for compatible memory modules.
- **PCI Express (PCIe) slot:** A motherboard expansion connector used by supported cards such as a GPU.
- **Power supply unit (PSU):** The component that supplies regulated DC power to computer components.
- **Volatile memory:** Memory that does not retain its contents when power is removed, such as standard system RAM.
- **Electrostatic discharge (ESD):** A sudden static-charge transfer that may damage electronic components.
- **POST:** Power-on self-test checks performed during startup before the operating system loads.
- **Thermal interface material:** Material placed between a processor and cooler to improve heat transfer, when required by the cooler instructions.
- **Troubleshooting:** A methodical process of identifying symptoms, testing likely causes, applying a suitable correction, and verifying the result.
